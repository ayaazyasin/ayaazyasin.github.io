Mug Image Cropper

← ayaazyasin.github.io

Upload an image, position it inside the print frame, and download the crop. The frame is locked to the mug wrap size of 11.89 × 5.52 in (horizontal).

1 · Choose an image

JPG, PNG, or PDF. Higher-resolution originals give a sharper print. For a PDF, the first page is used.

Drag to reposition · pinch, scroll, or use the slider to zoom. Everything inside the bright frame is exported.

2 · Download the crop

Exported at the full resolution of the area you selected.

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
